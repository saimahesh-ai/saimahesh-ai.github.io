Peer-Reviewed Conference Proceedings

S. M. Mudavat, A. Mitra, S. P. Mohanty, and E. Kougianos

"LiteViT: Leveraging the Power of Transformers for Edge AI in Crop Disease Classification"

Proceedings of the IEEE Computer Society Annual Symposium on VLSI (ISVLSI), 2025.

Impact: Compressed a 300M-parameter Vision Transformer to a 3.8 MB MobileViT-XXS model, achieving 99.3% accuracy at 12.26 ms inference latency — making state-of-the-art disease classification feasible on edge hardware for the first time.

"FLitViT: Vision Transformers-Based Federated Learning Approach for Plant Disease Classification"

Proceedings of the 23rd OITS International Conference on Information Technology (OCIT), 2025.

Impact: First federated learning framework to combine Vision Transformers with privacy-preserving distributed training for agricultural disease classification across decentralized edge nodes.

"DPRS: Secure and Lightweight Federated Vision Transformers for Agricultural Edge AI"

Accepted.

Impact: Introduced a Byzantine-robust federated framework with INT4/INT8 mixed-precision quantization, reducing bandwidth by 60% while sustaining 98.04% accuracy via a Temporal Checkpoint Manifold for automated failover.

"TinyVarNet: A Dispersion Gated Dual Kernel Network for Plant-Disease Classification"

Accepted.

Impact: Achieved 82.03% accuracy on cluttered real-field benchmarks with only 28K parameters — featuring parameter-free dynamic routing and intrinsic multi-scale spatial explainability for trustworthy edge deployment.

"E2-WeedNet: A Compact Equivariant Network for Shortcut-Aware Crop and Weed Recognition"

Accepted.

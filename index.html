About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate and process-driven researcher specializing in Trustworthy Lightweight AI, Computer Vision, and Edge Computing for Smart Agriculture. My work centers on designing lightweight, resource-constrained models for on-device and edge AI that are intrinsically explainable, and Byzantine-robust neural architectures that bridge the gap between high-capacity deep learning and resource-constrained field robotics.

I leverage a foundational industry background in scalable cloud orchestration and MLOps to ensure academic models are robust, deployable, and privacy-preserving. Moving forward, I am deeply interested in addressing challenges related to building hyper-localized AI models for agriculture, intercropping systems, and diverse agro-practices across different regions and environments.
